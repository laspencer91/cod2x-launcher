# Launcher artwork

The supplied gold CoD2x emblem is included with redistribution permission. It retains its original ownership. The launcher backdrop is original artwork made with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Backdrop prompt

> Create one original wide panoramic photographic backdrop for the main banner of a desktop CoD2x game launcher. Style reference from the supplied imagery: dark charcoal and warm muted bronze with an early-2000s wartime game atmosphere. An empty European coastal fortification from the 1940s at dusk, distant angular concrete walls, rough stone, ground fog, paper-grain texture, extremely subdued tungsten light and very dark shadows. No figures, no weapons, no flags, no symbols, no lettering or logo, no UI. Place the architectural interest on the right half and leave the left half very dark and low-detail for white UI text. The right half will also have a separate gold emblem over it, so keep most of the frame dark and atmospheric. Wide 3:2 landscape composition cropped to a wide banner, contemporary photographic realism, naturally lit, nearly monochromatic charcoal bronze.
